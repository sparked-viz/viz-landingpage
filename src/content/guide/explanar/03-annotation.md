# Annotation

Drawing on top of the canvas while explaining.

## Pencil

Click the **pencil** icon in the header to start drawing directly on the canvas. A floating panel appears with five stroke sizes (smallest to largest) plus a **Ban** icon above them for size 0 — pick a size and start writing.

![The Pencil Tool](/guide-images/annotation-pen-tool.webp "Activating the pencil from the header to draw on the canvas.")

![Pencil Sizes](/guide-images/annotation-pen-sizes.webp "Five stroke sizes, plus a size-0 option, in the floating panel.")

Undo and redo buttons at the bottom of the same panel step back through your strokes one at a time.

## Eraser

Click the **eraser** icon in the header to remove strokes instead of adding them. The same floating panel appears with five eraser sizes to choose from — bigger sizes clear more at once.

![The Eraser Tool](/guide-images/annotation-eraser.webp "Activating the eraser from the header.")

![Eraser Sizes](/guide-images/annotation-eraser-sizes.webp "Five eraser sizes in the floating panel.")
