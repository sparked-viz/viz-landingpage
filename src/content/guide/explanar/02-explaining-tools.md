# Explaining Tools

Tools for narrating and navigating the canvas while explaining.

## Ghosts (Preview the Current Step)

Click the **ghost** icon next to the speed selector to see what will play during the current step before you play it.

![Ghosts Off](/guide-images/explanation-mode-ghosts-off.webp "Only what's already visible shows on the canvas.")

![Ghosts On](/guide-images/explanation-mode-ghosts-on.webp "A faint preview of what the current step is about to reveal.")

## The Cursor / Pointer Tool

Click the **arrow** icon in the header to switch back to a plain cursor — use this to point at objects while explaining without drawing on the canvas.

![The Cursor / Pointer Tool](/guide-images/annotation-pointer.webp "Switching to the plain cursor from the header.")

## Zoom (Ctrl + Scroll)

Hold `Ctrl` (`Cmd` on Mac) and scroll to zoom in or out, centered on wherever your cursor is.

![Zooming In and Out](/guide-images/explanation-zoom-inout.webp "Ctrl + Scroll zooms toward the cursor.")

## Scrolling

Vertical: scroll. Horizontal: `Shift` + scroll.

![Scrolling the Canvas](/guide-images/explanation-mode-scroll.webp "Scrolling to move around the canvas.")

## Panning (Middle Mouse Button)

Hold the middle mouse button and drag to pan the canvas.

![Panning the Canvas](/guide-images/explanation-mode-pan.webp "Dragging with the middle mouse button to pan.")
