# Editing a Recording

## Split

Select an event on the timeline (or leave nothing selected to target the audio track), position the playhead where you want the cut, then click **Split** — or press `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `B`. The event or audio clip is divided into two independent pieces at that point.

![Splitting an Event](/guide-images/split-action-in-the-editing.webp "Selecting an event, positioning the playhead, and clicking Split.")

## Remove Silences

Sparked Viz highlights detected gaps of silence in the audio waveform. Click **Remove silence** to cut every highlighted gap out of the recording at once, shortening its total length. The button is disabled once there's nothing left to remove.

![Removing Silences](/guide-images/remove-silences.webp "Detected silence segments are highlighted, then removed with one click.")

## Rearranging Actions

Click an action on the timeline to select it, then drag from its middle to move it to a different point in time. Drag from either edge instead to trim how long it lasts.

![Rearranging Actions](/guide-images/rearranging-the-user-actions-in-editor-mode.webp "Dragging a selected action to reposition or trim it.")

## Export

Click **Export** to open the Export MP4 panel.

![Opening the Export Panel](/guide-images/click-herte-to-export.webp "The Export button opens the Export MP4 panel.")

Choose a **Resolution** (HD, Full HD, or 4K — Full HD and 4K require Premium; free exports are capped at 720p and get a 2-second "Made with Viz" end card), an **FPS** (24, 30, or 60), a **Quality** (Low, Ideal, or High), a **Pencil size** (0.5x–2x, scaling annotation strokes in the export), and whether to **Include audio**. Click **Export MP4** to render and save the file.

![Choosing Export Settings](/guide-images/select-the-quality-and-export.webp "Resolution, FPS, Quality, Pencil size, and Include audio settings before exporting.")
