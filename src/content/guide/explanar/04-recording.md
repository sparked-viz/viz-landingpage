# Recording

## Click Record

Click **Record** in the header to open the recording setup panel. A red viewfinder frame appears on the canvas showing exactly what will be captured, alongside a panel for choosing your microphone and aspect ratio before you start.

![Click Record](/guide-images/record-button.webp "The Record button in the header opens the recording setup panel.")

## Microphone Permission

The Microphone dropdown starts on "No microphone," with a message reading "Enable access to use audio." Click **Enable** to grant the browser access to your mic — until you do, no audio input is available to select.

![Microphone Permission](/guide-images/enable-microphone.webp "Click Enable to grant microphone access.")

## Selecting the Microphone

Once access is granted, the dropdown lists every audio input device connected to your computer. Pick one to use it for the recording.

![Selecting the Microphone](/guide-images/choose-the-microphone.webp "Choosing an input device from the microphone dropdown.")

## Selecting the Aspect Ratio

Choose **16:9**, **4:3**, **1:1**, or **9:16**. The red viewfinder frame on the canvas resizes to match, so you can see exactly what will be captured before you start recording.

![Selecting the Aspect Ratio](/guide-images/select-the-aspect-ratio-recording.webp "The four aspect ratio options — the camera frame updates to match your choice.")

## Start Recording

Click **Start recording** to begin. Sparked Viz jumps to the first step, switches into Explanar (presentation) mode, and automatically selects the Pencil tool so you're ready to annotate as soon as capture starts.

![Start Recording](/guide-images/start-recording.webp "The Start recording button begins capture.")
