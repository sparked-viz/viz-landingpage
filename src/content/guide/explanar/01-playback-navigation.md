# Playback & Navigation

Playing back, in Explanar, the lesson you built in the Editor. Flip the **Presentation** toggle in the header to leave the Editor and enter Explanar.

![Turning On Presentation Mode](/guide-images/turning-on-the-presentation-mode.webp "The Presentation toggle in the header switches from Editor to Explanar.")

Once you're in, a floating control bar in the bottom-right handles playback.

## Playing the Current Step

Click **Play** to play the current step's animations in order.

![Playing the Current Step](/guide-images/explanation-play-current-step.webp "The Play button starts the current step's animations.")

## Next / Previous Step

The skip-back and skip-forward buttons move to the previous or next step; the counter next to them (e.g. "1/4") shows where you are in the sequence.

![Going to the Next Step](/guide-images/explanation-go-to-next-step.webp "Skip forward to move to the next step.")

## Playing Fast or Slow

The speed selector next to the playback controls works the same as in the Editor — **0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, 2x, 4x, 8x,** or **16x**.

![Selecting a Playback Speed](/guide-images/explanation-select-playback-speed.webp "Choosing a playback speed for the current step.")
