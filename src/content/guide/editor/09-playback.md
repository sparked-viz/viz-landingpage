# Playback in the Editor

Previewing your work before moving on to Explanar.

## Playback Speeds (0.5x–16x)

Next to the **Actions** menu in the Steps panel, the speed selector controls how fast the current step plays back when you hit Play or drag the scrubber: **0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, 2x, 4x, 8x,** or **16x**.

![Playback Speed Selector](/guide-images/playback-speed.webp "Choosing a playback speed from 0.5x up to 16x.")

Slow it down to 0.5x to check the exact timing of a fiddly animation, or speed through a long step at 8x or 16x to get to the part you actually want to check.
