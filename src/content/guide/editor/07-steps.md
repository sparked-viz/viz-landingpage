# Steps

## What Are Steps?

A step is a self-contained beat of your explanation — its own small group of animations that play together, one step at a time. Instead of choreographing one long continuous timeline, you build a lesson as a sequence of steps, and presenting it in Explanar means playing through them in order, one beat at a time.

Open the **Steps** panel from the right dock to see and manage them.

![The Steps Panel](/guide-images/steps-panel.webp "The Steps panel, opened from the right dock.")

## Adding a Step

Click the **+** button at the end of the step pill row to add a new, empty step after the last one.

![Adding a Step](/guide-images/add-step.webp "The + button at the end of the step row adds a new step.")

## Navigating Between Steps

- Click any **step pill** to jump straight to the start of that step.
- Use the **Previous Step** / **Next Step** buttons to move one step at a time relative to where you are.
- The **Play** button plays through the current step's animations in order; once it reaches the end it turns into a **Replay** button.
- Drag the **playhead** along the scrubber below the pills to scrub through the current step's timing without leaving it.
- The speed selector (**1x** by default) controls playback speed for both the Play button and scrubbing.

## Reordering Steps

Open the **Actions** menu for the currently selected step and choose **Move left** or **Move right** to swap it with its neighbor. Move left is unavailable on the first step, and Move right is unavailable on the last.

![Reordering Steps](/guide-images/reorder-steps.webp "Move left and Move right swap a step with its neighbor.")

## Deleting Steps

Open the **Actions** menu and choose **Delete step**. This option is hidden when only one step remains — an explanation always needs at least one.

![Deleting a Step](/guide-images/delete-step.webp "Delete step removes the currently selected step.")

## Inserting a Step In Between

Open the **Actions** menu and choose **Add step before** or **Add step after** to insert a new, empty step next to the one you're on — unlike the **+** button at the end of the row, this lets you insert in the middle of the sequence.

![Inserting a Step In Between](/guide-images/add-step-inbetween.webp "Add step before / Add step after insert an empty step next to the current one.")

## Splitting a Step

Unlike inserting an empty step, **Split step** divides a step that already has animations in it. Drag the playhead to a point in the step where some animations end before it and some start after, and a **Split step** button appears right on the playhead — click it to break the step into two at that exact point, with the earlier animations staying in the original step and the later ones moving into a new one right after it.

![Splitting a Step](/guide-images/split-step.webp "Splitting a step at the playhead divides its animations into two steps.")

Split step only appears when the playhead sits between two animations — not before the first or after the last, and not if everything in the step falls entirely on one side of it.

## The Actions Menu

Next to the speed selector, the **Actions** menu gathers everything above into one place for whichever step is currently selected: **Add step before**, **Add step after**, **Move left**, **Move right**, and **Delete step**.

![The Actions Menu](/guide-images/actions-menu.webp "The Actions menu for the currently selected step.")
