# Animation Blocks

Fine-grained control over the animations inside a step. Each animation you add — whether from directly editing the object, the floating toolbar, or the magic wand — shows up as its own **block** in the Steps panel, and every block has a small **`...`** button opening a menu of further options.

## Deleting a Block

Click the block's **`...`** button and choose **Delete animation** to remove it from the step.

![Deleting an Animation Block](/guide-images/delete-animation-block.webp "The ... menu's Delete animation option removes a block.")

## Making a Block Faster or Slower

Click the block's **`...`** button and choose **Slower** or **Faster** — each click nudges its playback speed down or up in small steps.

![Changing a Block's Speed](/guide-images/make-animation-faster-slower.webp "Slower and Faster adjust a block's playback speed.")

## Rearranging Blocks

Drag a block by its card to reorder it within its lane — blocks in the same lane play one after another, in the order they're listed, so dragging one earlier or later changes when it plays relative to the others.

![Rearranging Animation Blocks](/guide-images/rearranging-the-animation-blocls.webp "Dragging a block to change its play order within a lane.")

## Running Animations Together

Drag one block on top of another to bundle them into the same slot — once bundled, both play at the same time instead of one after the other. This is the quickest way to make two animations run together without setting up a separate lane for them.

![Running Two Animations Together](/guide-images/runninig-together.webp "Dragging one block onto another bundles them to play simultaneously.")

## Creating a New Sequence

Drag a block onto the **new sequence** drop zone at the edge of the track list instead of onto another block, and it's placed in its own parallel lane — labeled **Seq A**, **Seq B**, and so on. Every lane in a step plays in parallel with the others, but each keeps its own internal order, so you can rearrange blocks within one lane without disturbing the rest.

![Creating a New Sequence](/guide-images/creating-new-sequence.webp "Dragging a block onto the new-sequence zone gives it its own parallel lane.")

## Moving a Block to Another Step

Drag a block's card up onto a different **step pill** in the row above the track list to move that animation into that step entirely.

![Moving a Block to Another Step](/guide-images/moving-animations-to-another-step.webp "Dragging a block onto a different step pill moves it into that step.")
